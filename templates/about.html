About AquaCare

Engineering safe drinking water for homes, businesses and industry.

Our story

From a single service van to a full manufacturing line

AquaCare began with a simple observation: most people were not let down by the purifier they bought, but by the service that followed. Our founders spent their early years repairing other manufacturers' machines and saw the same failures repeat — undersized membranes, generic spares and technicians who never returned.

So we started building our own systems. Today AquaCare designs, assembles and quality-tests every purifier we sell, then services it through our own field team. The engineer who installs your system belongs to the same company that manufactured it, which means accountability does not get lost between vendors.

We now support households, schools, restaurants, hospitals, offices and production plants — each with purification designed around their actual water report, not a generic brochure.

Our mission

To make genuinely safe drinking water affordable and dependable — by building purification systems that suit local water conditions and supporting them with service that actually shows up.

Our vision

A region where no family, classroom or workplace has to question the water in the glass — supported by efficient systems that waste less water and last longer.

Capability

Manufacturing, technology and quality

Manufacturing capability

Dedicated assembly bays for domestic units and a fabrication section for commercial and industrial skids up to 2000 LPH.

Quality assurance

Every unit passes leak testing, flow-rate verification, TDS reduction checks and electrical safety inspection before it is packed.

Technology

Thin-film composite RO membranes, high-output UV chambers, hollow-fibre UF and mineral cartridges — matched to your source water.

Customer satisfaction

Trackable service requests, published pricing and a service team measured on first-visit resolution, not call volume.
