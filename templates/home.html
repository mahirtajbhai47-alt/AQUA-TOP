Who we are

Water purification engineered for Indian conditions

AquaCare builds purification systems that handle real-world water — high TDS borewell supply, hard municipal lines and variable industrial feed. Every unit is assembled in our own facility, pressure-tested and certified before dispatch.

From a 7-litre kitchen RO to a 2000 LPH industrial plant, the same team designs the system, installs it at your site and keeps it running through planned maintenance and annual contracts.
